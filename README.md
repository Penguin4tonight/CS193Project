Project Goal: As a person who likes to plant tomatoes back at home, I want to build an application to track the lifecycle of my crops. When it comes to gardening the most important thing is staying on top of crop lifecycles and understanding when to harvest the fruits of the crops and how much time it has left. 
Build: To solve the issue of managing the lifecycle and harvest dates of crops I plan on building a react application that stores the current lifecycles of a person's crops. From that storage the app can then use the current date to decide when crops need to be harvested and what stage in the lifecycle they are in. 
Tech Stack: React, JSON or CSV(PapaParse or React-CSV-Reader), JavaScript, CSS, Html
MVP: An application that can add and retrieve crop data to a csv and based on that data, display the remaining crop lifespan and the current stage its in. 
Future Features:Colors to demonstrate lifecycle of Crops. Small Crop images to help individual when it comes to looking at site. Ability to display crop lifecycles from most urgent to least urgent.
